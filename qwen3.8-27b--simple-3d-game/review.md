Super impressive results! Of course, it has been using that sweet-sweet xhigh thinking, for nearly 10 out of 13 total HOURS! But the game is trully impressive. It looks, feels and plays very good.
A few minor issues:
- The high barrier that the game expects us to go under actually looks high enough to jump over, but the game doesn't let us, feels a bit weird but understandable.
- The overall idea of what appears to be a spaceship, that somehow manages to squeze down to the ground like it's made out of rubber is questionable. And the fact that the spaceship can jump over barriers is a bit weird too if you think about it, ship can fly in any direction, does no jumping... But since this is a simple game and not the most realistic, it's understandable.
Overall very very well done, but took a loooot of time.
