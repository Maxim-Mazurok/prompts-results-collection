Actually pretty good. First of all, it runs. Game mechanics are pretty advanced, there are waves of enemies, enemies take damage and drop health packs when defeated.
The issue is that aiming is backwards, towards the player, making it hard to aim, at first I thought that shooting just didn't work.
Then also enemies are hard to see (just red cubes) but like dark-red or transparent, or under the playing plane... Hard to tell. But eventually you can see then and hit.
Overall, not too bad, especially considering how fast it was.
